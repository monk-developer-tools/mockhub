A mock API in one click

You get your own URL on the demo. Start with two sample stubs, your API spec, a GraphQL schema, your own records or one of the examples, then change it and call it from your app or tests.

Two stubs to try: GET /hello and GET /users/{id} with a fake name and e-mail.

One stub per operation, with responses from your examples or from the schemas. Up to 500 KB and 20 operations; the first 20 are used. A URL must be public; a link ending in .wsdl or ?wsdl is read as WSDL.

You get a GraphQL endpoint at /graphql that answers every query and mutation in the schema with realistic generated data, plus an explorer here to try them. Up to 500 KB.

You get a full REST resource: GET, POST, PUT, PATCH and DELETE that keep what you send. Up to 100 records to start; the mock keeps up to 200.
