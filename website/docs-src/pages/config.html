Public tools

With MOCKHUB_PUBLIC_TOOLS=true, anyone can use these endpoints without signing in. They are off by default. When they are off, /__public/ is passed to your mocks like any other path.

GET /__public/headersThe request headers
ANY /__public/status/{code}Answers with that status
ANY /__public/delay/{ms}Answers after the delay, up to 10,000 ms

Limits per client address: 120 requests a minute to the tools, 1,200 requests a minute to bins (webhooks sent to them and their owners reading or changing them), 30 renders or fake-data calls a minute, 100 new bins an hour and 10 new instant mocks an hour. Over the limit the answer is 429 with Retry-After. Bins are stored under the data directory and deleted 24 hours after their last request; the server holds at most 1,000. A bin keeps every request until it holds 50 MB, then drops its oldest file of 10 MB, and all bins together stop at 4 GB.

The render endpoint turns off helpers that read the server (systemValue, hostname), partials and assign. It also caps loops at 2,000 runs in total, sizes and ranges at 10,000, rendering time at 2 seconds and output at 256 KB. Behind a reverse proxy, MockHub takes the client address from the last entry of X-Forwarded-For, the one your proxy adds, so a client can't choose its own. Set your proxy to add it (nginx: proxy_set_header X-Forwarded-For $proxy_add_x_forwarded_for;), and don't expose MockHub directly when you rely on these limits.

Instant mocks are projects in a private organisation called "Instant mocks". The server holds at most 200. Each has up to 20 stubs, answers 1,000 requests a day, keeps up to 200 CRUD records of up to 4 KB each, and is deleted 7 days after its last change. Stubs and request bodies are limited to 64 KB. To keep one mock from slowing down the others, instant stubs can't proxy, call out, send webhooks, read files or use regex, XPath or JSON schema matching, and delays go up to 10 seconds. Response templates render with the same limits as the render endpoint and a 1-second budget.

When public tools are on, users whose server role is below admin can't see the "Instant mocks" organisation, and the native WireMock admin API (/__admin/mappings and the rest) is for admins only, because it lists the stubs of every project.
