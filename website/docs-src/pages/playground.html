The Playground (sidebar) sends requests to your mocks or to public APIs from the server, so browser CORS rules don't get in the way. It shows status, time, size, headers and a formatted body, and can copy the request as cURL or save the response as a stub in any project you can edit. It needs the editor role.

  • History: your last 100 requests and responses, private to you. Values of Authorization, Proxy-Authorization, Cookie and Set-Cookie are hidden before saving, and bodies over 64 KB are cut short.
  • Own mocks: paths like /shop/orders and URLs on MockHub's own address go straight to the local mocks. /__admin is refused.
  • Safety: private, loopback, link-local and other internal addresses are refused so the server can't be used to reach your network. Set MOCKHUB_PLAYGROUND_ALLOW_PRIVATE=true to allow them, for example to call services on your intranet. Redirects aren't followed; responses over 2 MB are cut short; requests time out after 30 seconds.
